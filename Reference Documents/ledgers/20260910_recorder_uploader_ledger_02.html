Recorder & Uploader Ledger

Two roles get confused across the 7 resource models: the cataloguer, who compiled and organized the data, and the Arches ingestor, who uploaded the record into the system. Both are people, and both are now linked to the Actor model. This is the settled field‑by‑field spec.

Renamed from Uploader name and converted to link to Actor, 2026‑09‑10 — same reasoning as Recorder: the ingestor is a person too, and should be a real Actor record. Consequence: Operational Info moves from Auto‑Populated to Foreign Key, since Arches can't resolve a login session to an Actor record on its own — entry becomes manual, like Recorder. Kept at Cardinality 1 (one ingestor per upload, unlike Recorder's 1..n).
