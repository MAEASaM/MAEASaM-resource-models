Recorder & Uploader Ledger

Two roles get confused across the 7 resource models: the recorder, who compiled the data itself, and the uploader, who entered the record into Arches. This is the field-by-field spec for both, with what's settled and what still needs a look.

This node records the name of the individual responsible for creating the resource record.

Datatype settled and applied (2026‑09‑09): Grid, Map and Chronology now link to Actor via Resource‑Instance‑List, matching RS/Info. Cardinality standardized to 1..n everywhere — the list widget already lets one card hold several recorders, so this just documents that multiple people can share credit.

Still open: which of the three field‑name variants becomes canonical — Recorder / Name of recorder / Name of the recorder. Not a call to make solo — for the team.
