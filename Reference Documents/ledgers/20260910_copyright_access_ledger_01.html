Info's outlier

A single merged field where everyone else has five
1 FIELD
Copyright Open question
TypeString
NecessityStrongly recommended
Cardinality0..1

Op. InfoN/A

Some kind of statement about copyright or other rights and restrictions associated with the resource (or a hyperlink to such a statement).

Info compresses Licence + Access level + Rights holder + Rights Notes into one free‑text field, under its own group name ("Copyright/access", lowercase, also inconsistent with RS/Chronology/Map's "Copyright/Access"). Not a bug to fix mechanically — a real question: does Info's content genuinely not need the granularity (no licence vocabulary, no Actor‑linked rights holder), or was it just never brought up to the same standard as the others?
